# AI use disclosure statement

Adapt one of these and place it in the methods section whenever AI made or
informed a judgment. See `references/ai-use-reporting.md` for what qualifies.

---

## Full version (AI used in screening and extraction)

> **Use of artificial intelligence.** We used [tool name, version, provider]
> between [start date] and [end date] at the following stages.
>
> *Search.* [Tool] drafted Boolean strategies from our concept blocks. All
> strategies were revised by [role], peer reviewed using PRESS by [role], and
> tested against [n] known eligible records. The strategies as run are
> reproduced in Supplement [x].
>
> *Screening.* Titles and abstracts were screened independently by [n] human
> reviewers and, in parallel, by [tool]. Every disagreement between the human
> reviewers and the tool was adjudicated by [role], and a random [x] percent of
> records on which they agreed were re-checked by a human. On a pre-screened
> validation sample of [n] records, tool sensitivity was [value] and specificity
> [value].
>
> *Data extraction.* [Tool] performed a first-pass extraction into a piloted
> form. Every field entering an analysis was verified against the source
> publication by [role]; [n] discrepancies were found and corrected.
>
> *Risk of bias and certainty.* Judgments were made by human assessors. [Tool]
> was used to [locate relevant methods text / draft domain rationales], which
> [role] verified against the source before a judgment was recorded.
>
> *Interpretation.* The synthesis, conclusions, and all interpretive text are the
> authors'. [Tool] was used only for [language editing / structuring drafts].
>
> *Citations.* All references were verified programmatically against Crossref and
> OpenAlex for existence, metadata consistency, and retraction status on [date];
> [n] were corrected or removed as a result.
>
> The authors take full responsibility for the content of this review, including
> all AI-assisted components. This use is reported in line with the RAISE
> recommendations and the 2025 joint position statement on AI use in evidence
> synthesis.

## Short version (limited, non-judgmental use)

> **Use of artificial intelligence.** [Tool name, version] was used on [dates]
> for [specific purpose, for example drafting database-specific translations of
> the search strategy, which were verified by an information specialist]. No
> eligibility, extraction, risk-of-bias, or certainty judgment was made or
> suggested by an AI tool. All references were verified against Crossref and
> OpenAlex for existence and retraction status on [date]. The authors take full
> responsibility for the content.

---

## No-AI version

> **Use of artificial intelligence.** No generative AI tool was used in the
> conduct of this review. Automation was limited to [reference management and
> deduplication in <named tool, version, settings>]. All references were verified
> against Crossref and OpenAlex for existence and retraction status on [date].

Name the automation even here. Deduplication software makes inclusion-relevant
decisions, and readers should know which tool and what settings.

---

## What to avoid

- **"AI was used in the preparation of this manuscript."** Names nothing,
  describes nothing, protects nobody.
- **Disclosure in the acknowledgements only**, when the use touched a judgment.
  It belongs in the methods, where the method is.
- **A tool name without a version.** Behavior changes under a stable product
  name, so an unversioned name does not identify what actually ran.
- **"Two independent reviewers" when one was a model.** Whether an AI second
  screener counts as independent screening is contested. Describe exactly what
  happened and let the reader judge.
