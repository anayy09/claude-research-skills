# Review protocol template

Complete this before screening begins, and register it. A protocol written after
screening is documentation, not preregistration, and registry timestamps make
the difference visible.

Registration: PROSPERO for health-related reviews with a health outcome, OSF
Registries otherwise. Scoping reviews are not eligible for PROSPERO; register on
OSF and say where.

## 2. Rationale and objectives

- What is already known, and which existing reviews cover it. Cite them. If a
  current systematic review already answers this question, justify doing another.
- The gap this review addresses.
- Objective stated as an answerable question using a framework (PICO, PICo,
  SPIDER, PIRD).

## 4. Information sources and search

- Databases with platforms, registries, preprint servers, grey literature.
- Other methods: forward and backward citation chasing, author contact, hand
  searching.
- Draft strategy for at least one database. Final strategies for every source go
  in the report, per PRISMA-S.
- Who peer reviewed the strategy (PRESS), and the known-item test set used.
- Planned date for the pre-submission search update.

## 5. Selection process

- Screening software.
- Number of reviewers at title/abstract and at full text; independent or not.
- Pilot: screen an initial set together and measure agreement before proceeding.
- Disagreement resolution procedure.
- Any AI assistance and the human verification procedure. See
  `references/ai-use-reporting.md`.

## 6. Data collection

- Extraction form, and how many studies it was piloted on.
- Number of extractors; independent or verified.
- Items extracted. Use CHARMS for prediction model reviews.
- Procedure for missing data and for contacting study authors.

## 7. Risk of bias

- Tool per included design: RoB 2, ROBINS-I, QUADAS-2, PROBAST+AI, AMSTAR 2.
- Any tailoring of signaling questions.
- Number of assessors and the adjudication process.
- How judgments will be used in the synthesis: restriction, sensitivity
  analysis, or GRADE downgrading. An appraisal that never affects a conclusion
  was not used.

## 8. Synthesis

- Effect measure per outcome.
- Whether meta-analysis is planned, and the criteria for deciding to pool.
- Model and estimator, and which heterogeneity quantities will be reported
  (tau-squared and a prediction interval, not I-squared alone).
- Prespecified subgroups and their rationale. Anything not listed here is post
  hoc when it appears.
- Plan when pooling is inappropriate: narrative synthesis reported against SWiM.
- Publication bias assessment, and the minimum number of studies for it to be
  informative.

## 9. Certainty of evidence

- GRADE per outcome; GRADE-CERQual for qualitative findings.
- Who rates and how disagreements resolve.
- Which outcomes appear in the summary of findings table.
